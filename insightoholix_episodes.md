# Insightoholix Podcast: Episode Guide

Channel: [youtube.com/@insightoholix](https://www.youtube.com/@insightoholix) · Hosts: Lalit Sharma and Vandana

This guide covers the channel's **12 full-length podcast episodes** (newest first). Teasers, highlight clips and Shorts are not included.

**How this was put together**

- **Title, date, duration and views** come from YouTube's public data, pulled on 25 October 2026. View counts will have moved since then.
- **Summaries, quotes and clip picks** come from each episode's full transcript. Most episodes are in Hindi or Hinglish, so the quotes are translated into English, keep the speaker's meaning and are lightly condensed.
- **Timestamps** come from YouTube's auto-generated captions and are accurate to within about 30 seconds. Start a clip a few seconds early when you cut it.
- **Claims about astrology, numerology and health** are the guests' own views, as said on the show.

## 5. Women's Health Secrets Doctors Rarely Tell You | PART 1 | Periods, PCOS, IVF & Menopause

| | |
|---|---|
| **Published** | 13 October 2025 |
| **URL** | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LAlagU6xhUE |
| **Duration** | 48:24 |
| **Views** | 395 |
| **Guest** | Dr. Poonam Gupta, obstetrician and gynaecologist |
| **Host** | Vandana |

**Summary**

Vandana opens a two-part series on a topic "surrounded by taboos, myths and half-knowledge." Dr. Poonam Gupta talks about choosing obs-gynae and balancing an always-on job with family. She explains the four phases of the menstrual cycle and why a "normal" cycle can be anywhere from 21 to 35 days. She covers the causes of irregular periods (an immature hormonal axis in teenagers, PCOS, thyroid, prolactin, stress, weight change) and how PCOD differs from PCOS, a condition she calls a "new-age pandemic." She gives practical red flags for painful and heavy periods. The fertility half covers pre-conception counselling and folic acid three months before trying, how to calculate your real fertile window, the biological clock after 35, social egg freezing (cost about ₹1.2–1.5 lakh per cycle plus ₹30–40k a year for storage), ovarian reserve tests, premature ovarian insufficiency and smoking. She explains when to see a doctor (after 12 months of trying, or six months if over 35) and the step-by-step path from ovulation induction to IUI to IVF, and busts common IVF myths.

**5 key quotable lines / insights**

1. **[6:01]** "A normal cycle isn't exactly 28 or 30 days. It varies from woman to woman, anywhere from 21 to 35 days. If it comes every 21 days regularly, that's normal for her."
2. **[8:31]** "Our textbook used to have a small column on PCOD. Now it's a huge topic. I'd call it a new-age pandemic."
3. **[24:32]** "Couples should understand there is a biological clock in women which is ticking day by day. Prioritise your career, yes, but take this into consideration too."
4. **[27:30]** On social egg freezing: "It freezes your fertility for a time, so you don't have to worry about the ticking clock. But think about it a little earlier, around 25 to 30, not at 35 or 40."
5. **[42:00]** "The common misconception is that one IVF means 100% success. Even in the best of hands the chance is maybe 40, 50, 60%, not more."

**3 strong short-clip moments**

1. **[5:30–7:02] "Is my cycle normal?"** The 21–35-day rule and when to worry. It answers one of the most-searched women's health questions in about 90 seconds.
2. **[27:00–31:03] Egg freezing explained, with real costs.** Who it's for, the ideal age, ₹1.2–1.5 lakh per cycle and ₹30–40k a year for storage, and the fact that a 25-year-old's egg stays 25. Highly searchable and shareable.
3. **[42:00–43:01] Three IVF myths busted.** It's not 100% success, one failure doesn't mean the next will fail, and it doesn't always mean twins. Short, punchy and myth-busting.

## 6. I Lost ₹3 Lakhs in 3 Months Trading – Here's What No One Tells You

| | |
|---|---|
| **Published** | 28 September 2025 |
| **URL** | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sDGD1Q-FGDk |
| **Duration** | 19:58 |
| **Views** | 234 |
| **Guest** | A retired bank employee who began trading after retiring (name not given in the episode) |
| **Host** | Lalit Sharma |

**Summary**

After retiring from his bank job in March 2022, the guest tried selling insurance, found friends and relatives avoiding his calls, and turned to the stock market to stay busy from home. Stocks went well: he built a profit of about ₹4 lakh by June. Then a ₹1,500 YouTube class and one lucky week (about ₹17–18k profit) pulled him into options. The second week he lost ₹49,000, and for July, August and September he lost more than ₹1 lakh each month. He traded with no stop-loss, target or strategy. He averaged into losers with ever bigger quantities and once sold calls without understanding the margin. He also made 29 trades in a day without realising brokerage was eating most of his profit. He explains how fear and greed played out on single trades and what he changed: smaller lots, a cap of five trades a day, Nifty only, and fewer but better trades. He closes with advice for anyone thinking of trading full-time.

**5 key quotable lines / insights**

1. **[3:01]** "Whatever I earned in stocks, options made it all even." (₹4 lakh of stock profit wiped out in about three months)
2. **[8:02]** On a trading video he watched: "He said, whoever doesn't know stop-loss, swing high, swing low, only God can help him. And I realised I didn't know any of it either."
3. **[9:01]** "I had a ₹60,000 loss sitting on the chair. I couldn't bear it. I cut the position and lay face-down on the bed for two minutes. Then I got up and said, it's happened, it's happened."
4. **[15:30]** "One day I made 29 trades and was happy with ₹6,500 profit. Three days later I checked the P&L: ₹5,000 had gone in commission. ₹1,500 profit."
5. **[19:01]** "Whether you trade three times a month, five or ten, make them quality trades. If you trade five days and stay out for 25, that is also profitable for you."

**3 strong short-clip moments**

1. **[0:00–1:00] The cold open.** A fast montage: the YouTube class, the "only God can help you" line, the ₹60,000 loss and lying on the bed, "more than ₹1 lakh lost every month for three months." It's already cut like a Short.
2. **[9:01–10:30] The ₹60,000 afternoon and the margin shock.** A raw, relatable account of a big loss, followed by the discovery that selling 300 quantity of calls had locked up his whole margin.
3. **[11:31–13:31] Fear vs greed on a single trade.** A ₹11,000 profit that he wouldn't book slides to ₹8k, ₹5k, zero and then a loss. Later a ₹60,000 stop-out that would have recovered by 3:30 pm. Classic, highly shareable trader psychology.
