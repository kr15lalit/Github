## 7. Instagram revival: what to post first

**Don't open with a "We're back!" post.** Nobody engages with it, and it tells new visitors you were gone. Lead with your best content.

**Days 1–3: build the grid before you announce anything.**
New visitors decide in about 3 seconds, based on your top row. Make sure the first 3 posts are strong, then **pin** them:
1. 📌 The Satsang widow Reel (emotional, proven topic)
2. 📌 The "Is your period normal?" carousel (saves, credibility)
3. 📌 The Sweden ₹0 hospital bill Reel (relatability)

**Days 1–30: cross-promote from YouTube (where your 14.7K already are)**
- **Community post on day 1:** "We're now posting daily clips on Instagram 👉 @insightoholix. Follow for the moments you missed."
- **Pinned comment** on your top 5 videos (Satsang, Numerology, Sweden ×3): "Daily clips from our conversations on Instagram: @insightoholix 🎙️"
- **YouTube description template:** the first line after the summary is "📲 Instagram: instagram.com/insightoholix"
- **Every Reel's caption** ends with: "Full conversation on YouTube, link in bio."
- **Each Sunday highlight video:** post a Story with the link sticker → "New 5-min story on YouTube 🎬"

**Guest collab posts (your biggest untapped lever)**
Use Instagram's **Collab** feature so the Reel appears on both your profile and the guest's:
- Dr. Arun Tungaria (*NeuroMate Talks*, active on Instagram) → the Week 2 sleep Reel
- Dr. Poonam Gupta, Sanddeep Bajaj, Nitisha Malhotra → their Reels
- Message them 3 days before: "We're posting your clip on [date]. Can we add you as a collaborator?"

A single collab can bring more followers than a month of solo posts, because their audience already trusts them.

**Engagement routine (15 minutes a day, non-negotiable)**
- Reply to **every** comment within the first hour after posting
- Comment meaningfully on 5 posts from similar Indian podcasts or creators
- Reply to every Story poll or quiz response that has text

### C1: "Is your period normal? 5 signs to see a doctor" (Women's Health P1)

4. **Common causes:** PCOS · thyroid · high prolactin · sudden weight gain or loss · stress
5. **See a doctor if your period:** is so painful it disrupts your day · lasts over 7 days
