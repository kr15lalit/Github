Find a swing trade, step by step

A repeatable process: check the market, pick strong sectors, screen for leaders near support, check the chart, then plan the trade before you buy.

Open pullback screen → Open breakout screen →

These buttons open the screener with every scan, filter and sort from Steps 3 and 5 already applied.

No setup guarantees a gain. Even good swing setups often win only about half the time. You make money because winners are bigger than losers, so Steps 7 and 8 matter as much as finding the stock. Educational process, not investment advice.

  1. 1Check the market 10 seconds

    Look at the Market Health tiles at the top of the screener:

    • Above 200 EMA at 50% or more, with its arrow pointing up.
    • Above 20 EMA rising.

    If both are weak and falling, take no new trades, or only very small ones. Most swing trades fail in a falling market.

  2. 2Find the strong sectors

    Click Sectors and note the top 5 that have:

    • Avg RS above 60
    • % > 20 EMA above 60%
    • CMF above 0 (money flowing in)

  3. 3Set up the screen

    Back on Stocks (or just use the Open pullback screen button above):

    SettingValueWhy
    Market capLarge, mid or smallSkip micro caps until you're experienced
    ScansStage 2 (6+/7) + RS LeadersConfirmed uptrends, top 20% performers
    Turnover ≥10 ₹ CrEasy to buy and sell
    Max % above 20 EMA5Near support, not extended
    Within % of 52W high−15Near its highs
    RSImin 50, max 70Healthy momentum, not stretched
    SortRS, highest firstStrongest at the top

    Prefer stocks from your Step 2 sectors. Save the screen with My filters → Save current screen so tomorrow it's one click.

  4. 4Check each chart

    Click the first stock, then use the ← → keys to flip through the rest. Keep a stock only if:

    • It's in Stage 2 on the chart: price above a rising 50 and 200 EMA, with higher highs and higher lows.
    • The pullback is calm: small candles and low volume as it dips toward the 20 or 50 EMA. Big red volume bars are a warning.
    • The Weekly view agrees: switch to Weekly and the trend should look up there too.
    • There's a clear support level just below the price (20 EMA, 50 EMA or a recent swing low). That's where your stop will go.
    • RSI is about 50–65 and turning up (green dot).
    • Next earn. is not within the next 2 weeks. Results can wipe out a trade overnight.

    Click ✨ AI analysis for a second opinion on the trend, levels and risks.

  5. 5Choose your setup type

    A. Pullback to supportFrom the pullback screen
    1. Wait for the stock to touch or come near the 20 EMA.
    2. Enter when it closes above the previous day's high: buyers stepping back in.
    B. Fresh breakoutFrom the breakout screen
    1. Scan Breakout stocks with RS ≥ 70 and Stage 2 ≥ 5, sorted by BO vol.
    2. Only take BO vol ≥ 1.5x and BO gain% under 5%.
    3. Enter near the breakout level; avoid one that slipped back below it.

  6. 6Shortlist

    Add the best 3–5 to a watchlist (the coloured dots). Rename one "Ready" in the Watchlists panel. Write a note for each with the entry trigger, stop level and target.

  7. 7Plan the trade before you buy

    ItemRule
    Stop-lossJust below the support level (20 EMA, swing low or breakout level), ideally 5–8% below entry at most
    TargetAt least 2x your risk. Risk 5% → aim for about 10%
    Position sizeRisk at most 1% of capital per trade. Use the calculator below

    If the stop has to be too far away to make sense, skip the trade.

    Position-size calculator

  8. 8Manage the trade

    • Exit immediately if your stop is hit. No hoping.
    • At 2x your risk: sell half and move the stop on the rest to your entry price.
    • Trail the rest: exit if it closes below the 20 EMA (the 10 EMA for fast movers).
    • Exit early on a high-volume drop, or if the market's health tiles turn sharply negative.

The 10-point checklist

0/10Tick what the stock passes

Look for at least 8 of 10 before trading. Your ticks reset when you leave the page.

Daily habit · 15 minutes

Each evening: run your saved screen, check the charts, update the watchlist, and set alerts or orders for the next day. Practise on paper for a few weeks before using real money.
