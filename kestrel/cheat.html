How the levels are calculated

  • Entry trigger: just above the latest session's high. For a pullback, a close above the previous day's high shows buyers stepping back in.
  • Stop-loss (pullbacks): 1% below the lower of the 20-day EMA and the lowest low of the last 5 sessions.
  • Stop-loss (breakouts): 2% below the breakout level (the top of the base it broke out of).
  • Targets: 2x and 3x the risk (entry minus stop). Quantity risks your chosen % of capital.
  • Order rules: buy with a stop-loss limit (SL-L) or GTT order at the trigger, limit 0.5% higher. Don't buy above entry + half the risk (gap-ups worsen reward-to-risk). Each order is valid for 3 sessions, and is cancelled if price hits the stop first. Place the stop-loss as soon as you're filled.
  • Score: the playbook's 10-point checklist, checked automatically. Still open the chart and check the pattern yourself (Step 4 of the playbook).
Rule-based screen output, not investment advice. These are mechanical calculations from end-of-day data, not recommendations to buy or sell. They can be wrong, gaps can jump past stops, and past patterns don't guarantee future results. Do your own research, and consult a SEBI-registered adviser before investing.
