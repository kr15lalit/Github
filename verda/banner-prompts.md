# Hero banner prompts (ChatGPT image generation)

How to use:
1. In ChatGPT, upload clear front photos of the real Verda packs shown in the banner.
2. Paste the shared rules once, then the prompt for one banner. Make one banner per chat message.
3. Check the result:
   - packs look like ours
   - no FSSAI or legal text is visible
   - every word is spelled correctly
4. Send the image to Claude. Claude adds the official Verda logo, crops the image to the exact size and puts it on the site.

## Shared rules (paste first)

```
I'm attaching photos of my brand's real product packs (Verda). Create a premium,
photo-realistic e-commerce banner using these packs.

Packaging rules:
- Reproduce the packs faithfully: same shape, colours, label layout, the Verda name
  and the product names as on the reference photos. Do not invent new pack designs.
- Show only the FRONT of each pack, facing the camera or slightly angled.
- Do NOT show any FSSAI logo, licence number, barcode, MRP/price, batch number, dates,
  nutrition table, ingredients list, address or any other small legal text. Where such
  text would be on the pack, leave the label area plain.
- Do not add any product that is not in my reference photos.

Style rules:
- Warm natural daylight, rustic Indian kitchen props (wooden board, brass/steel bowls,
  jute cloth), raw ingredients next to each pack, shallow depth of field.
- Cream / beige background tones with deep green (#1f5f23) and gold (#9c6b12) accents.
- Leave the empty area described below completely clean: no logo, no watermark.
  I will add my official logo myself.
- Text in the image must be spelled exactly as given, in an elegant serif font,
  dark green, and nothing else written anywhere.
- Keep all packs and text away from the outer edges (about 8% margin), because the
  banner will be cropped slightly on different screens.
```
